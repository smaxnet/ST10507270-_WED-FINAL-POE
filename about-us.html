Our Story

Built on South African Flavour

Kota Bite started with a simple idea: bring the best of the South African kota to a modern, welcoming space where people can enjoy generous portions, bold flavours and great value.

From our classic fillings to our crispy sides and refreshing drinks, every order is made with care and served with the warmth and friendliness that customers expect from a trusted local food brand.

We believe food should not only satisfy hunger—it should create memorable moments. Whether you are grabbing a quick bite, ordering for the family, or treating yourself after a long day, Kota Bite is here to make that moment delicious.

Our Mission

To serve fresh, flavourful food with quality, consistency and community at heart.

Our Values

Fresh ingredients, generous portions, honest pricing and memorable service.

Our Promise

Every kota is made to satisfy cravings and bring people together around food.
